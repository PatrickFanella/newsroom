# Automatic Twitch shows and manual correction

On the Shows page, the owner can enable automatic shows after linking Twitch and configuring the application's public HTTPS EventSub callback. The app registers `stream.online` and `stream.offline` subscriptions together before enabling the setting. **Check Twitch subscriptions** reads their current status. **Repair automatic-show subscriptions** explicitly replaces only this app's online/offline subscriptions for the owner's broadcaster; chat subscriptions and other broadcasters are untouched. Repair may be needed after changing the callback or EventSub secret.

Automation defaults to off. Signed, fresh lifecycle notifications are persisted atomically with their show changes before acknowledging Twitch. Stable delivery and stream IDs prevent duplicate shows or reopening a manually ended stream. A timestamp watermark and stream-ID checks stop late offline notifications from ending a newer show. Manual active shows are preserved. If a newer online event arrives after a missed offline, only the previous automatic show is closed at the new start, and affected notes are reassigned. Overlapping historical boundaries are reported as needing correction rather than overwritten.

Online start time comes from Twitch's `started_at`. Offline has no end timestamp field, so the signed EventSub message timestamp is used as an approximation. These fields follow the [official subscription definitions](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/eventsub/eventsub-subscription-types/) and [event reference](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/eventsub/eventsub-reference/). The rundown snapshot is captured when the event is processed. Enabling while already live does not manufacture an online event; use manual start until the next real online notification.

Disabling automation does not end the active show or delete provider subscriptions; subsequent notifications are ignored. Use manual End when needed. Only the owner can change automation settings, and revision checks prevent a slow registration response from overwriting newer settings.

Under **Show boundaries**, choose **Correct boundaries** to edit UTC start/end times. Corrections require the loaded show revision, cannot overlap another show or exclude a recorded play, and recalculate chapter offsets for assigned notes. Drafts survive failed saves. **Load latest boundaries** explicitly replaces the draft with current data. Clearing an ended show's end reopens it under manual control. Public episode snapshots and saved Discord exports remain unchanged until explicitly republished or exported as another version.

Apply `20261014140000_automatic_twitch_broadcasts.sql` after broadcast sessions and before app deployment. Existing shows stay manual. Local tests use synthetic signed-envelope validation, mocked subscription operations, and disposable PostgreSQL lifecycle transactions; they do not register or send real Twitch events. Live callback delivery and provider authorization require verification in the deployed environment.
