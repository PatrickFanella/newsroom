Where will your newsroom live?

Use a domain you own. In your domain provider’s DNS settings, point its A record to your server’s public IP. If you add an AAAA record, it must point to this server too.

We’ll start Caddy to obtain and renew your website’s certificate. Your domain must point to this server, with ports 80 and 443 open and available.

Advanced: app port

The app is accessible only on this server at this port. Keep 3000 unless another app uses it.

How will you sign in?

Connect an email service that supports SMTP. It sends the private sign-in links. Use the SMTP settings supplied by your provider; an app password may be required instead of your account password.

Use an address your email provider allows you to send from.

Leave username and password empty only if your SMTP relay does not require authentication.

Open Twitch’s developer console ↗ and register an application. Use the callback address below as its OAuth redirect URL, then copy its client ID and generate a client secret.

The installer generates the webhook signing secret and encryption key for you. Chat features need a publicly reachable HTTPS website.
